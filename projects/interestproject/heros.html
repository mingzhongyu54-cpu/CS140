MingZhong Yu's Hero: Beethoven

Interests Heros

Beethoven is a very well-known composer amoung the music world. I have been playing piano for almost half of my life, I had the opportunities to perform many pieces, out of all the pieces i played, being able to play one of his composition always brings me joy. Beethoven is my hero because of his incredible musical talent and the way he overcame adversity to create masterpieces that still inspire people (such as me) today.

Famous Pieces
9th Symphony, Moonlight Sonata, Pathétique, Für Elise
Achievements
He was awarded the title of "The Great Composer", Awarded
Personal Characteristics
Determined, Passionate, Creative, he was deaf later in his life yet he managed to compose the 9th Symphony
