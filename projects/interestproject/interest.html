I like the feeling of creating melodies and the satisfaction of mastering new pieces, especially pieces by Beethoven and Chopin


Video Games

Aside from music and piano, I also enjoy playing video games in my free time. MMORPGs are my favorite type of video game. However, I have recently developed a liking towards Indie games, only downside is that most indie game cost money to play. I like the creativity and unique storytelling found in many MMORPG and indie games.
