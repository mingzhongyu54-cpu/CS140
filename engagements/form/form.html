What are different ways to style your form? I can change the colors, size and margin of the form, 'fieldset' can also be used to edit the form.

Why does the professor like to use flex? Flex makes it easier to line up labels and fields and control the spacing between them.

What makes for a good mobile form? A good mobile form is easy to read and use on a small screen. It should have clear labels, fields that are large enough to tap, and a layout that fits the screen.
